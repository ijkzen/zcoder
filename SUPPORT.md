## 提 issue 之前 / Before opening an issue

1. 先搜索[已有 issue](https://github.com/ijkzen/zcoder/issues)，确认没有重复。
2. 提供环境信息：手机型号与系统版本、应用版本（`0.4.1+8`）、桌面端版本。
3. 缺陷报告请附复现步骤；涉及连接的请附日志（`adb logcat`，或桌面端 `~/.zcode/v2/logs/`）。

1. Search [existing issues](https://github.com/ijkzen/zcoder/issues) first.
2. Include your environment: device model & OS version, app version (`0.4.1+8`), desktop version.
3. For bugs, provide reproduction steps; for connectivity issues, attach logs (`adb logcat`, or the desktop's `~/.zcode/v2/logs/`).
